About Us

Who We Are · Thirty Years of Kept Promises
"Thirty years of kept promises."

Exports Partner is a professional India-based sourcing and export service provider dedicated to helping overseas buyers procure products from reliable Indian suppliers. A Mumbai-based sourcing and trading house with 30+ years of experience, a reliable supplier network and a global export reach.

Quality products are backed with lab certification, offered at the best prices, and sampling is offered before you commit to volume. Business terms run on Bank L/C & Escrow payment thru bank — and global delivery moves via air cargo, ocean ship & air courier.

✅ What We Specialize In
  • Product sourcing from verified manufacturers
  • Factory coordination & negotiations
  • Quality inspection & packaging supervision
  • Export documentation & shipment handling
  • Complete order fulfillment support
